## Acceptance rule

This is the same rule as Phase 2 (`docs/audits/PHASE2_PLAN_2026-11-02.md`). A capability becomes RELEASE_READY only through `src/suite/commands/verifiedCommands.ts`. Its binding must meet all of these:

- Every clause of the requirement text has passing checks.
- Those checks drive the real UI, save the result, and reopen it independently with PDF.js and pdf-lib.
- They run in CI.
- The PR has a clean review from Claude and from Codex.

Requirements that cannot honestly meet their text stay unbound, with the gap stated.

## Slice 3.1 — Forms (MAL-046 – 050, 052, 053)

### Engine: `src/suite/pdf/forms/`

**`formDesign.ts`** creates, lists, moves and resizes, renames and deletes AcroForm fields:
- Field types: text, number, date, check box, radio group, dropdown, list box and unsigned signature placeholders.
- Fields are placed from rectangles drawn on the page as shown, for any page or view rotation.
- Widgets get `/MK /R`, so appearances are drawn upright on rotated pages.
- Radio buttons carry their label in their own appearance, so a label moves with its button.
- Choices store export values and labels as `/Opt` pairs; the appearance shows the label.
- Signature placeholders hold a `/Lock` (`SigFieldLock`) with All, Include or Exclude.
- Signed or certified documents are refused.

**`formLogic.ts`** writes the logic as standard Acrobat form scripts:
- `AFNumber_Format`/`Keystroke`;
- `AFDate_FormatEx`/`KeystrokeEx`;
- `AFRange_Validate`;
- `AFSimple_Calculate`;
- a pattern check;
- a `display.visible`/`display.hidden` toggle.

It also orders `/CO` by dependency and refuses cycles. It reads these statements back with exact patterns only, and evaluates them itself.

**`formFill.ts`** fills a form with its logic applied:
- Every validation problem is reported, and the fill is refused until they are fixed.
- Calculated values are written.
- Number and date fields keep the raw value in `/V` and show formatted text in the appearance.
- Fields hidden by a rule get the Hidden widget flag.
- Appearances that a reader would otherwise have to draw (choice labels) are drawn.

It also flattens all fields or chosen ones into page content. Hidden widgets are dropped. A field that another remaining field depends on is refused.

**`formData.ts`** exports and imports CSV (`name,value` rows, or a header row of names), JSON, FDF and XFDF:
- Import matches full field names and accepts labels in place of export values.
- It reports unknown names, and skips read-only and calculated fields.
- XFDF with a DOCTYPE or ENTITY is refused.

### UI

- **Forms → Design form…** (`PdfFormDesignerPanel`):
  - Pick a type and its settings, choose **Place on the page**, then drag.
  - Click a field's outline to select it.
  - **Move or resize by dragging**, rename, edit properties and logic, or delete.
  - Per-field settings reset after each placement; the type and number/date format carry over.
- **Forms → Fill form…** (`PdfFormFillPanel`):
  - Live calculation, visibility and per-field errors. **Save values** is disabled while any error remains.
  - Flatten selected fields or all fields.
  - Export and import CSV, JSON, FDF and XFDF.

### Judgement calls (flagged for the owner)

1. **MAL-050 "sandboxed supported JavaScript actions".** MALENJO does not execute document JavaScript at all. The supported actions are the standard AF functions listed above, written so other readers apply them. MALENJO recognises them by exact pattern and evaluates them in its own code. Any other script is kept in the file, listed in the designer as "not run", and never executed. This is stricter than a JS sandbox, and it is the safe reading of "sandboxed supported".
2. **MAL-049** creates signature *placeholders* and their properties (tooltip, required, lock list). Applying a digital signature belongs to the signing requirements.
3. **MAL-053 "where applicable".**
   - Signature and push-button fields have no data to exchange and are left out.
   - CSV multi-selections are repeated `name,value` rows.
4. **Formatting.** Values are stored raw (as readers do), and the formatted text appears in the appearance stream. Flattening therefore shows the formatted value, for example `$1,250.50`.

## Slice 3.2 — PDF to images and text formats (MAL-029, 036, 041 – 044)

### Engine: `src/suite/pdf/convert/`

- **`imageCodecs.ts`** has its own encoders:
  - PNG: RGB or RGBA, with the DPI in `pHYs`.
  - TIFF: baseline, little-endian, RGB or RGBA (unassociated alpha), uncompressed or Adobe Deflate, single or multi-page.
  - JFIF density for JPEG.

  It also handles background removal by colour and tolerance (with a soft edge), region cropping, and PDF.js image data to RGBA.
- **`rasterExport.ts`** renders with PDF.js at the chosen DPI (36–600). The canvas is transparent when asked, then cropped to a dragged region, keyed for background removal, and encoded.
- **`pdfStructure.ts`** reads text items (with bold and italic from font names) and images from PDF.js, then builds:
  - lines, split into cells at wide gaps;
  - tables, from consecutive multi-cell lines whose cells share column starts;
  - lists, from bullet and number markers;
  - headings, from document-wide font-size ranks and bold single lines;
  - paragraphs, with de-hyphenation.

  It also provides the three plain-text reading orders: as stored, top-to-bottom/left-to-right, and columns (gutter detection, with spanning lines splitting sections).
- **`textFormats.ts`** writes Markdown (GFM tables, images as `assets/*.png`), HTML (structured, or positioned text and images in points), and plain text with page separators (blank line, nothing, form feed, or a custom line with `{n}` and `{total}`).
- **`svgExport.ts`** builds on the slice-7 content model:
  - paths (m, l, c, v, y, h, re) with fill, stroke, even-odd and width under their CTM;
  - text as `<text>`, sized to the PDF advance with `textLength`;
  - JPEG images and 8-bit Flate RGB/grey images with soft masks.

### UI

**Convert → Export as…** (`PdfExportDialog`):
- Choose the format and pages (or the current page only).
- Images: DPI, JPEG quality, transparency, background removal, a dragged region (PNG), TIFF compression, and multi-page TIFF.
- Markdown/HTML: images, and the HTML layout.
- Text: reading order and separator.

One file downloads as itself; several files download as a ZIP that includes `export-report.txt`. An export report is shown after every export.

### Judgement calls

5. **Structure is reconstructed heuristically** from layout and font sizes, because most PDFs carry no tag tree. The export report says so on every Markdown and HTML export.
6. **SVG "eligible graphics / supported vector constructs".** These are left out and named in the report:
   - form XObjects;
   - shadings and patterns;
   - clipping paths;
   - dashes;
   - colours set through colour-space operators;
   - image formats other than JPEG and 8-bit Flate RGB/grey.
7. **MAL-044 "OCR-recognized text"** means the text layer that OCR adds to a PDF. That includes invisible text (render mode 3), which the acceptance check covers. Running OCR itself is MAL-027.

## Slice 3.3 — PDF to Office, batch conversion, result editors (MAL-037 – 039, 045)

### Engine

`src/suite/pdf/convert/officeExport.ts` builds on the slice 3.2 structure model:

- **DOCX**
  - Styles: Heading1–6, ListParagraph and TableGrid.
  - Real numbering: bullets, and decimal numbering restarted for each list.
  - Paragraph runs with size, bold and italic.
  - Bordered tables with a bold header row.
  - Inline PNG images.
  - Each new PDF page starts with a page break before its first paragraph, so no empty paragraph is inserted.
- **PPTX**
  - One slide per page, at the first page's size.
  - Text boxes at their positions; consecutive lines that share a left edge become one box.
  - Pictures.
  - Filled or outlined rectangles from the content model (`re`, or a closed four-corner path).
- **XLSX**
  - One sheet per detected table, with the header row bold and numbers stored as numbers.
  - Pages without a table get their lines as rows.
  - Column widths come from the content.

Every result carries fidelity notes.

### UI and workflow

- **Convert → Convert to Office…** (`PdfOfficeConvertDialog`):
  - Profiles: four built-in, plus saved ones, each holding format, pages and images.
  - Several PDFs, with a per-file fidelity report; failures say why.
  - Successful results are opened in the editors.
  - **Download results (ZIP)** includes `conversion-report.json`.
- **App** (`openConversionOutputs`) registers DOCX, XLSX and PPTX results as browser documents and opens each as a tab in the existing Word, Spreadsheet or Presentation result editor. These remain conversion-result editors, not import surfaces; the direct-open policy is unchanged.
- **The DOCX result editor** now separates paragraphs by exactly one blank line, so empty paragraphs (empty table cells) keep their place and a text correction can be exported.

### Judgement calls

8. **Office fidelity.** Conversion is reconstruction, not lossless, and every result's report says what was rebuilt and what was not. Some things are not reconstructed:
   - multi-column flow;
   - footnotes;
   - non-rectangle vector drawings in PPTX (counted in the report);
   - embedded fonts (Arial is used at the original sizes).
9. **Result editors.** They are the existing basic editors: DOCX paragraph text, XLSX cells, PPTX slide text. They support review, correction, undo/redo and export, as the spec's "basic review, correction, editing, saving, and export" requires.
